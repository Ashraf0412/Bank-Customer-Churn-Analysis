Key Insights
  • Germany has the highest churn rate followed by France and Spain.
  • Customers aged 51+ are the most likely to churn.
  • Inactive customers churn at over twice the rate of active members.
  • Single-product customers show the greatest churn risk.
  • Higher credit scores correlate with lower churn.
