About Project

Bank Customer Churn Insights - is an interactive analytics dashboard designed to help banks monitor customer behavior, identify churn patterns, and support data-driven retention strategies. Built using -Python, SQL, and Power BI-inspired visualizations, the system analyzes customer demographics, credit scores, account balances, product usage, and activity status to uncover key trends. It also includes a rule-based Risk Insights - module that prioritizes customers based on multiple risk factors, enabling early intervention and informed business decisions through dynamic, data-driven visualizations.
